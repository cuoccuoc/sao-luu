Chinh sach rieng tu

Ung dung "Sao luu may tinh" chi duoc dung boi chinh chu so huu de sao luu file tu may tinh ca nhan len Google Drive cua chinh nguoi do.

Ung dung chi truy cap cac file do no tao ra tren Google Drive (quyen drive.file). Khong thu thap, khong luu tru o noi khac, khong chia se hay ban du lieu cua bat ky ai.

Privacy policy (English): this is a personal, single-user backup app. It only accesses files it creates in the owner's own Google Drive and does not collect, store elsewhere, share or sell any user data.
